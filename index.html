NASA PSI Data Citations

Experimental telemetry, flammability thresholds, and emergency suppression insights are compiled from the NASA Physical Sciences Informatics (PSI) open-science data repository (NASA Biological and Physical Sciences Division, Glenn Research Center & ISS National Laboratory).

Repository: NASA PSI · Combustion Science
SAFFIRE-I, II & III [1–6]

Spacecraft Fire Experiment (Saffire) aboard Northrop Grumman Cygnus vehicles. NASA Glenn Research Center; NASA PSI Combustion Datasets for large-scale Sibal composite, PMMA, and Nomex flammability limits in concurrent and opposed low-speed flow.

BASS-II & SoFIE [7–10]

Burning and Suppression of Solids II (Microgravity Science Glovebox) and Solid Fuel Ignition and Extinction (Combustion Integrated Rack, ISS). NASA PSI empirical datasets mapping solid-polymer Goldilocks flammability boundaries.

ACME Investigation Suite [11–23]

Advanced Combustion via Microgravity Experiments (BRE, s-Flame, Flame Design, CFI-G, CLD Flame) conducted in the ISS Combustion Integrated Rack (CIR). NASA PSI records covering elevated O2 burning, spherical radiative extinction, and cool diffusion flames.

FLEX, FLEX-2 & Cool Flames [24–31]

Flame Extinguishment Experiment and FLEX-2 droplet combustion investigations in the ISS CIR Multi-User Droplet Combustion Apparatus. NASA PSI datasets on radiative extinction, gaseous suppressants (CO2, He, SF6), and dual-zone cool flames.

SPICE [32–34]

Smoke Point In Coflow Experiment aboard the ISS Microgravity Science Glovebox. NASA PSI optical pyrometry and soot volume fraction archives quantifying laminar coflow smoke points in 0g relative to 1g normal gravity.
